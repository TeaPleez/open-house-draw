Open House Draw: Official Contest Rules

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Who runs this draw

This draw is run by Dylan Jones, eXp Realty (the "Host"). Questions: dylan@homeswithdylan.ca.

2. Who can enter

  • You must be 19 years of age or older.
  • Real estate agents and other real estate licensees, and members of the Host's immediate family, are not eligible.
  • You must enter in person at an open house hosted by the Host. If a person is entered into the draw at a location other than an open house, the max ticket limit is 1.

3. How to enter

No purchase is necessary. Complete the entry form at the open house with your name, phone number and email address. Accurate information is required.

  • Standard entry: 1 ticket.
  • Mini-Putt winner: 3 tickets. Win the Mini-Putt game at the open house and the Host will confirm and enter your 3 tickets.

Limit of one entry per person per open house.

4. The draw and the prize

A draw is held each time 250 tickets have been entered. One winner is chosen at random from all of the tickets in that draw, so the odds of winning are 1 in 250 per ticket.

The prize is $50 CAD, paid by Interac e-Transfer. There is one prize per draw. The prize is not transferable and has no substitute.

5. Winner notification and claiming

The winner will be contacted by phone or email, using the details on the entry form, within 48 hours of the draw. The winner has 2 days from being contacted to respond and claim the prize. If the winner cannot be reached or does not claim the prize within 2 days, the prize is forfeited and the Host may draw an alternate winner.

To claim the prize, the winner may be required to correctly answer a mathematical skill-testing question.

6. General conditions

Entries with false, incomplete or duplicate information may be disqualified. The Host is not responsible for lost, late or incomplete entries or for technical problems. The Host may change or end the draw at any time, and any changes will be posted on this page. The Host's decisions about the draw are final. This draw is governed by the laws of British Columbia and the federal laws of Canada.

7. Your personal information

How your information is used is explained in the Privacy Notice.
